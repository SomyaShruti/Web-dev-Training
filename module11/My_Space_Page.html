Fun Space Facts

The largest volcano in the solar system is Olympus Mons on Mars.

Jupiter has the shortest day of all the planets, completing a rotation in just under 10 hours.

The Milky Way galaxy is estimated to contain 100-400 billion stars.

Neutron stars are incredibly dense, with a sugar-cube-sized amount of material weighing about a billion tons on Earth.

Saturn's rings are made up of ice and rock particles, ranging in size from tiny grains to large boulders.
