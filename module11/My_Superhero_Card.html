Cat Woman

Her claws are out.The mask is off.

Super Powers

Cat Woman has super agility and can climb walls with ease. She is also an expert in hand-to-hand combat and has a keen sense of smell.

She is known for her stealth and cunning, making her a formidable opponent.

She is also a master of disguise and can blend into any environment.

Weaknesses

Cat Woman's main weakness is her vulnerability to certain types of attacks, such as those that exploit her agility and stealth. She can also be overconfident at times, which can lead to mistakes in judgment.

Additionally, her strong sense of justice can sometimes cloud her judgment and lead her into dangerous situations.
